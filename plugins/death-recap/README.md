# PvP Tracker

Your fights against other players: what you did, and who killed you. Death Recap until 0.2.0; the PvP tab is Nullception's K/D/A Counter.

- **PvP:** your kills, deaths and assists against players since the last reset, your streak (assists count toward it; only dying ends it), your KDA, and the Abyss Points you earn while in the Abyss. A sound for each kill climbs as you chain them, up to six in a row, with a sparkle on top after that. Sounds, volume, and opening the window when you enter the Abyss are in Settings, off until you turn them on.
- **Last death:** after a player kills you, their name, legion, server, class, level, combat power and gear score, the damage they did to you, in how many hits, the fight's length and their DPS, and the skills they used; and everyone else who damaged you in that fight (your party never can).
- **History:** every death kept; click one to see it again.
- **Tally:** deaths counted by player, legion or server.
- **Popup:** a corner toast when you die (or a centre banner, a slim strip, or none). Drag it to move it; click it to open the window.

Nothing about other players reaches this plugin while you live: the PvP counts carry no names, and who killed you comes only after the death.

Needs A2Tools DPS Meter 2.0.94 or newer for the PvP tab (the meter's `kda` event), 2.0.81 for the death recap.
